    From PDF to firefight in three steps.

    Multiplayer

    Your browser is the server.

    Peer-to-peer over WebRTC. Single player opens no connections at all; the room only exists while the host keeps the tab open.

    Host

    Drop your CV, then press Esc.

    1. Type your name and press Invite friends.
    2. Copy the link and send it. Up to 7 friends.
    3. Keep the tab open: closing it ends the game.

    Join

    Open the link, type a name, Join game.
